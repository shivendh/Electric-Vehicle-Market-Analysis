# 🔋 Electric Vehicle Market Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project provides a comprehensive analysis of the electric vehicle (EV) market using available data. It explores trends in EV adoption, market size, segment distribution, and growth patterns — helping stakeholders, analysts, or researchers understand the dynamics of the EV market and forecast potential expansion.  

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Analyze EV market growth and adoption over time  
- Examine market segmentation (by vehicle type, geography, or other attributes)  
- Identify trends in EV popularity, demand, and potential growth areas  
- Visualize EV market insights for easier interpretation and decision-making

## 🧪 Analysis & Methodology  

- **Data Collection & Preprocessing** — Load raw EV data, clean missing or inconsistent entries, normalize formats.  
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** — Explore distribution of EV registrations, sales, types (BEV, PHEV, etc.), regional/segment distribution, year-on-year growth.  
- **Market Sizing & Trend Analysis** — Estimate total addressable market (TAM), growth rates, penetration rates — study adoption trends over time.  
- **Segmentation Analysis** — Segment EV market by vehicle type, region, customer demographic (if data permits) to analyze preferred segments / high-growth areas.  
- **Visualization & Reporting** — Use charts, graphs, dashboards to present insights and trends clearly.  

---

## 📈 What This Project Delivers  

- Year-wise and region-wise EV adoption and growth trends  
- Market size estimates and growth rate calculations  
- Breakdown of EV market by type (e.g. BEV, PHEV), segments or categories  
- Visualizations (charts/graphs) summarizing key insights  
- Data-driven insights that can support business decisions or research on EV-market potential
